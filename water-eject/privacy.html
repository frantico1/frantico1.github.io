Water Eject: Audio Toolkit — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

Publisher: Ali Büyükaksım (alikasim). Contact: 06buyukkasim@gmail.com.

Microphone and recordings

The app requests Android microphone permission only for Microphone Test and Sound Meter. Microphone Test creates a temporary recording in the app's private cache for playback. It is deleted when you leave the test or choose Delete. Sound Meter processes microphone samples on your device and does not save them. Microphone recordings and samples are never uploaded or shared by the app. You can deny or revoke microphone permission in Android Settings; the other audio tools remain available.

Local preferences

Your appearance choice, default Water Eject mode and completed-session count are stored on your device. The app has no user account, developer backend or separate analytics service. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes these local preferences and cached files.

Advertising and Google services

The app displays Google AdMob banner and interstitial ads. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic/performance information, advertising ID, app set ID and other applicable device/account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. These data are encrypted in transit. Microphone audio is not provided to the advertising SDK.

Google's User Messaging Platform manages applicable ad consent and privacy choices. Where required, the app asks for your choices before requesting ads. You can revisit available ad privacy choices from the app's Settings. Android's advertising settings let you reset or delete your advertising ID. Ad availability and data use depend on your region, consent choices and device settings.

Google's handling and retention of advertising data are governed by its privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Learn about advertising controls: https://myadcenter.google.com/. The publisher does not store these advertising data on a separate server. Contact us with privacy questions or requests; Google service data can also be managed using Google's privacy controls.

Children

This general-purpose audio utility is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13.

Contact and changes

For questions about this policy, contact 06buyukkasim@gmail.com. We will update this page and its effective date when our data practices change.

Mikrofon ve kayıtlar

Uygulama yalnızca Mikrofon Testi ve Ses Ölçer için Android mikrofon izni ister. Mikrofon Testi, dinleyebilmeniz için uygulamanın özel önbelleğinde geçici bir kayıt oluşturur. Testten ayrıldığınızda veya Sil seçeneğine bastığınızda kayıt silinir. Ses Ölçer, mikrofon örneklerini cihazınızda işler ve kaydetmez. Uygulama mikrofon kayıtlarını veya örneklerini internete yüklemez ve paylaşmaz. Mikrofon iznini Android Ayarlar'dan reddedebilir veya kaldırabilirsiniz; diğer ses araçları kullanılabilir olmaya devam eder.

Yerel tercihler

Görünüm tercihiniz, varsayılan Su Çıkarma modu ve tamamlanan işlem sayısı cihazınızda saklanır. Kullanıcı hesabı, geliştirici sunucusu veya ayrı bir analiz hizmeti yoktur. Uygulamayı kaldırmak veya depolama alanını temizlemek bu tercihleri ve önbellek dosyalarını siler.

Uygulama Google AdMob banner ve geçiş reklamları gösterir.
